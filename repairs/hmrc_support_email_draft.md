Subject: regulation_url truncated at 200 characters; all 13 affected legal acts return 404

Hello,

We maintain an open data-quality suite for UK trade remedy measures and found a
reproducible defect in the Trade Tariff API that your team is best placed to fix.

## Summary

The `regulation_url` field on legal acts served by the Trade Tariff API appears to be
capped at 200 characters. Thirteen legal acts cited by live trade remedy measures carry
a URL of exactly 200 characters, cut mid word, and every one returns HTTP 404. Every
URL in the same field shorter than 200 characters resolves with HTTP 200, and no URL
exceeds 200 characters, so this looks like a column or serializer length limit rather
than bad source data.

## Reproduce

```
curl -s "https://www.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk/api/v2/commodities/7208100000" \
  | jq '[.included[] | select(.type=="legal_act") | .attributes.regulation_url | select(length==200)]'
```

Any commodity carrying measures generated by recent trade remedies notices shows the
pattern; their gov.uk paths are the longest in the dataset, so the cap lands almost
entirely on them. Across the full tariff, 13 of the 49 legal acts cited by trade remedy
measures are affected, which reaches 1,477 of 9,385 live measures (15.7 percent).

## Repaired values

We rebuilt each full URL from the gov.uk collection page in the surviving prefix; each
truncated URL extended to exactly one candidate, and each candidate returns HTTP 200.
The full before-and-after table (CSV) is here:

https://github.com/fabio-rovai/trade-remedy-ontology/blob/main/repairs/legal_act_url_repairs.csv

Affected legal act IDs: N2200100, N2300030, N2300080, N2300140, N2300150, N2300160,
N2300220, N2300230, N2300260, N2400150, N2500010, N2500070, N2500200.

Happy to open a PR against the data pipeline if you point me at where the field length
lives, or to provide anything else useful.
